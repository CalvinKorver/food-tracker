Food Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-26

Food Tracker stores your account email, meal diary entries, custom foods, goals, and preferences on our servers so you can use the website and iOS app.

Camera and photos

If you use Scan Label, the app captures a nutrition-label image and sends it to our servers, which call OpenAI vision models to extract nutrition fields. Images are processed to complete your request and are not used to train our own models.

Barcode

Barcode scans look up packaged products via Open Food Facts through our API. The barcode value is sent to our servers for the lookup.

AI connectors

Optional MCP / OAuth access lets you authorize AI clients to read or log diary data with scopes you approve. You can revoke access by signing out connected clients or deleting your account.

Your controls

  • Export your data from More → Account in the app.
  • Delete your account from More → Account (permanent).
  • Request a password reset from the sign-in flow.
